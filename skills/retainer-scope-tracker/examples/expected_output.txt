## Method and caveats

- Margin is `(retainer fee - delivery cost) / retainer fee`, where delivery cost is logged hours priced at each person's internal cost per hour. It excludes overhead, software, media and pass-through costs, so true account margin is lower.
- Flags fire at: hours over scope by more than 10.0%, hours under scope by more than 15.0%, margin below 55.0%, or effective rate more than 15.0% below the contracted rate.
- Only time entries dated inside the reporting month are counted. Work logged late lands in the month it was logged.
