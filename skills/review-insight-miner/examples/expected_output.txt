METHOD
  Vocabulary: terms in at least 3 documents and at most 35% of them (55 terms kept).
  Themes: terms merged by average pointwise mutual information at or above 1.0 bits, then every document assigned to its best-matching cluster by inverse document frequency.
  Categories come from a signal lexicon applied AFTER clustering; they label a theme, they never create one.
  Rating drag = (overall mean rating - theme mean rating) x rated reviews in the theme, in star-points.
  A negative drag means the theme rates ABOVE the overall mean: those clusters are praise, not a problem.

DEFECT VERSUS EXPECTATION
  product defects (the item is failing):        24 document(s)
  expectation mismatch (the page is failing):   14 document(s)
  logistics, service, usability, value:         28 document(s)
  no recurring theme:                           34 document(s)
  Defect themes cost margin through replacements and returns; expectation themes are usually cheaper to fix and fix faster, because the change is copy, spec, and photography.

========================================================================================================
Themes are statistical groupings of shared vocabulary, not a reading of intent. Sentiment is not modelled: a theme mixes praise and complaint about the same subject, so read the verbatims before acting.
