Subject: Scope creep starts in the kickoff

Scope creep doesn't arrive in month three. It gets signed in week one, in the kickoff deck nobody reread.

## Write the "not included" list first

Every SOW lists what you'll deliver. Few list what you won't. That second list is the one that saves you in month three, because it turns "can you just" into a change request instead of an argument.

**Steal this:** add a five-line "out of scope" block to your next SOW and read it aloud at kickoff.

## Change requests need a price, not a feeling

If a change request doesn't have hours and a number attached, it's a favor. Favors compound. Clients aren't being difficult when they ask; they just can't see the cost unless you show it.

**Steal this:** quote every change in hours, even the small ones, even if you waive the fee.
