Subject: Your utilization number is lying to you

Most agencies track utilization weekly and act on it quarterly. By then the burnout has already happened.

## Billable hours aren't the same as booked hours

A designer booked at 100% isn't busy. She's out of slack. The moment a client sends one extra round of revisions, something else slips, and it's usually the internal work nobody tracks. We see this in almost every agency that moves to Harborline: utilization looks healthy right up until two people quit in the same month.

**Steal this:** cap planned bookings at 85% and treat the rest as revision buffer.

## Status meetings are a symptom

If your Monday status call runs past 30 minutes, the meeting isn't the problem. The project board is. It can't answer "what's late and who's on it" without a human narrating it.

**Steal this:** make every task's owner and due date visible on one screen before you cut the meeting.
