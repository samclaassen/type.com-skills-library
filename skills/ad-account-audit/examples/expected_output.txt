# Paid account audit — Aurora Bikeworks — Google Ads (US/CA)

Period 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-21 (21 days) | 9 campaigns (8 enabled) | $16,583 spend

## Spend at issue

| Bucket | Amount | % of spend | What it means |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Wasted spend | $608 | 3.7% | Spend on traffic that converted zero times and can be blocked outright. |
| Unmeasured spend | $3,150 | 19.0% | Spend in campaigns with no recorded conversion. Not proven waste - it is unproven either way until tracking is fixed. |
| Unspent capacity | $4,030 | 24.3% | Budgeted capacity the account did not deliver against in the period. |
| Spend at risk | $7,290 | 44.0% | Spend exposed to a disapproval, an uncapped budget, or creative long past its refresh date. |
| Unattributable by naming | $780 | 4.7% | Spend in campaigns that break the naming convention, so it cannot be rolled up automatically. |

Conversion tracking coverage: 80.3% of spend sits in campaigns with at least one recorded conversion.

Buckets overlap by design — one campaign can appear in several — so do not add them together. Only the wasted-spend bucket is money that can be stopped outright.

## Findings (14)

### 1. [HIGH] No conversions recorded on $3,150 of spend — $3,150 unmeasured spend

- Campaign: `AB_US_PMax_AllProducts`
- Check: Conversion tracking
- AB_US_PMax_AllProducts spent $3,150 across 4,200 clicks and reported zero conversions.
- Every efficiency metric for this campaign is undefined until a conversion action fires.
- Fix: Confirm the conversion action is enabled, set as a primary goal, and included in this campaign's bidding. Re-audit after one full conversion window before judging performance.

### 2. [HIGH] Every enabled ad is disapproved - the campaign cannot serve — $2,100 spend at risk

- Campaign: `AB_US_YouTube_Awareness`
- Check: Ad approvals
- All 2 enabled ads are DISAPPROVED: ad-101001, ad-101002.
- The campaign holds $100.00/day of budget ($2,100 over 21 days) that cannot be delivered; actual spend was $120.
- Fix: Fix the policy violation or rebuild the ads. Until then, move the budget to a campaign that can serve instead of leaving it stranded.

### 3. [HIGH] Delivering only 5.7% of its daily cap — $1,980 unspent capacity

- Campaign: `AB_US_YouTube_Awareness`
- Check: Budget pacing
- Daily cap $100.00 x 21 days = $2,100 of capacity; actual spend was $120 ($5.71/day).
- $1,980 of committed capacity went undelivered in the period.
- Fix: Check bid caps, auction eligibility and audience size. If the campaign genuinely cannot absorb the budget, move the difference to a channel that can rather than reporting it as delivered.

### 4. [HIGH] $235 on search terms no negative blocks — $235 wasted spend

- Campaign: `AB_US_NonBrand_Search`
- Check: Negative keywords
- 2 search terms above $50 converted zero times and are not blocked by any negative on this campaign or the account list.
  - "how to fix a flat bike tire": $140.00 across 55 clicks, 0 conversions
  - "bike mechanic salary": $95.00 across 30 clicks, 0 conversions
- That is $235, or 5.3% of the campaign's $4,410.
- Fix: Add the shared intent tokens as phrase negatives at the campaign level, and promote anything account-wide (jobs, salary, free, DIY repair) to the shared negative list so new campaigns inherit it.
