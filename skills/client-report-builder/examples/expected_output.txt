===== Scenario 1: no finance definitions file =====

> **No finance definitions file found** (looked for `finance/revenue_definitions.json`; pass `--definitions <path>` to use one). This report uses the skill's own defaults, which are not finance's definitions:
> - Attributed revenue: each platform's claimed conversion value as exported, shown per platform and never summed.
> - Attribution windows: whatever each platform export used; not verified.
> - MER: net_sales / total_marketing_spend, with net sales from `--finance-totals`; not computed without it.
> - New-customer CAC: total_marketing_spend / new_customers, with new customers as counted in `--finance-totals` (no finance definition of a new customer); not computed without it.
> - Total marketing spend: paid media spend in the paid export.
> - Blended CAC: paid spend / every analytics conversion. Blended ROAS: analytics revenue / paid spend.
> - Currency: one currency as exported, shown as $; no FX conversion.

## What we're changing next month

1. **Cut TikTok Ads budget by 30% and rebuild targeting** (P1) — Platform-claimed ROAS of 0.67x is below the 1.51x floor on $5,200 of spend. Pulling $1,560 out funds the channels that are already clearing the floor.
2. **Fix conversion tracking before spending further** (P1) — Pinterest Ads spent $900 and reported zero conversions. Every efficiency number for this channel is unusable until the conversion action fires, so treat the spend as unmeasured, not as proven waste.
3. **Rebuild the worst-performing Meta Ads segments** (P2) — Cost per conversion moved from $29.36 to $36.05 (+22.8%). Prune the bottom-decile ad sets and refresh creative before raising spend.
4. **Cap Meta Ads daily budgets** (P2) — Meta Ads ran $1,500 over its committed $14,000 (110.7% of plan). Reset daily caps so the next cycle reconciles to the committed total.
5. **Recover $550 of unspent Microsoft Ads budget** (P2) — Microsoft Ads delivered 72.5% of its committed $2,000. Raise daily caps or widen targeting so the committed budget actually ships.
6. **Scale Google Ads by $4,320** (P3) — Google Ads claims 4.21x against a median platform-claimed ROAS of 3.02x across paid channels. A 20% increase is the largest step that historically holds efficiency within one reporting cycle.
