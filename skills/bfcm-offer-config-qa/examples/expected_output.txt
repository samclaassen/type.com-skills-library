  The FTC's Guides Against Deceptive Pricing (16 CFR 233.1) treat a former price as genuine when the item was openly and actively offered at it for a reasonably substantial period in the recent, regular course of business. This check flags a compare-at the SKU wasn't sold at on at least 30 of the 90 days before it was set. State laws can be stricter.
