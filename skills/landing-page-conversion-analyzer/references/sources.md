# Sources and provenance
Reference review date: 2026-09-10. The knowledge pack is original editorial guidance with short attributed summaries of the public sources below. External full articles, paid research, and customer data are not bundled. These sources inform diagnostic practice; none validates a specific customer's conversion performance.

## Checkout usability
Baymard Institute, How to Reduce Cart Abandonment:
https://baymard.com/learn/reduce-cart-abandonment
Supports investigating cost clarity and unnecessary checkout effort. Used in landing-page-review.md. Research across other stores supplies hypotheses, not a benchmark or diagnosis for this store.

## Experiment data quality
Microsoft Research, Diagnosing Sample Ratio Mismatch in A/B Testing:
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/diagnosing-sample-ratio-mismatch-in-a-b-testing/
Supports checking expected versus observed experiment allocation and diagnosing mismatch before trusting results. Used in experiment-playbook.md.

## Performance definitions
Google web.dev, Web Vitals:
https://web.dev/articles/vitals
Defines LCP, INP, CLS and their good-experience thresholds at the 75th percentile. Used in landing-page-review.md. Thresholds may evolve; recheck before a future technical audit. These thresholds are unrelated to the analyzer's historical CVR baselines.

## Editorial material
cro-foundations.md, experiment design guidance beyond the attributed SRM summary, templates/experiment-brief.md, and worked-examples.md are authored for this public edition. Worked numbers are synthetic teaching examples. Statistical implementation remains documented in methodology.md.
