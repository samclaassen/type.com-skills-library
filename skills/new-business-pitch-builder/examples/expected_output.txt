## How these numbers were built

- Every gap is valued in gross profit: $85.00 average order value x 55.0% gross margin = $46.75 per additional order. Paid-efficiency gaps are valued as recovered spend, not as profit.
- Each gap carries a confidence haircut reflecting how reliably that class of work lands: funnel step 35.0%, paid conversion rate 50.0%, paid efficiency 40.0%, paid tracking 60.0%, seo position 30.0%.
- Organic estimates assume a move to position 3 (11.0% click-through) and the site's current 1.39% conversion rate. Keywords beyond position 20 are excluded.
- Funnel gaps are each computed with the other steps held at today's rates. Fixing more than one compounds, so the combined effect is larger than the sum shown here — we size them separately to keep each claim independently checkable.
- Each paid channel contributes at most one gap. Where a conversion-rate gap and an efficiency gap describe the same channel, only the larger is counted and the other is shown as an alternative framing.
- Benchmarks come from the benchmark file supplied with this analysis, not from the prospect's own history.

## What this analysis cannot see

- Platform-reported conversions and revenue are used as given. Attribution overlap between channels is not removed, so channel-level revenue double counts to an unknown degree.
- No incrementality testing: none of these figures prove the paid channels caused the conversions they claim.
- Seasonality, inventory, pricing, competitive response and brand demand are all outside the data provided.
- '2 person tent' (https://tidewateroutfitters.example/collections/tents) sits at position 27, beyond the position 20 cutoff for a credible 90-day estimate.
