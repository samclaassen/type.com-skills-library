# User Research Synthesis — Workspace onboarding interviews, September 2026

_Internal research report · 7 participants · 7 interviews · Sep 14, 2026 – Sep 22, 2026 · synthesised Oct 2, 2026 · participants are pseudonymous (P1…); redaction is pattern-based, so read it before sharing._

**At a glance** — 3 research questions; 5 findings supported by 2+ participants (1 high, 3 medium, 1 low confidence); top opportunity by evidence: O2 (5 participants): Let admins invite the colleague who holds the credentials to connect the first data source. 2 items kept separate as single-participant signals.

## Research questions

- **RQ1** How do new admins get their first workflow live, and where do they stall? — 2 findings
- **RQ2** What do admins expect from the first-week setup checklist? — 3 findings
- **RQ3** Who else gets involved before a workspace is rolled out to the team? — 0 findings

## Findings by research question

_Confidence counts distinct participants, never mentions. Answers to leading questions are weaker and do not count towards medium or high. See Method for the thresholds._

### RQ1 — How do new admins get their first workflow live, and where do they stall?

#### F1. New admins stall at connecting the first data source, before they build any workflow. — High confidence

Support: 5 of 7 participants (P1, P2, P3, P4, P5) · 1 only via leading questions (P3) · Contradict: 1 (P7) · Said: P1, P2, P3, P4, P5 · Did (observed): none

By segment (supporting/participants): Self-serve admins 3/4 (1 contradict) · Sales-assisted teams 2/3

_Note: stated only; no observed behaviour._

- “I got stuck on the very first screen, connecting our shipment database.” — P1, Sep 14 · [M1](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-01)
- “Connecting the patient-booking system. The API key lives with our IT contractor, so everything stopped there.” — P2, Sep 15 · [M2](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-02)
- “The first week was mostly waiting on credentials for the scheduling system.” — P5, Sep 18 · [M5](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-05)
- Contradicting: Note excerpt (notes, not verbatim speech): _She connected the print-queue database herself in about ten minutes using the setup guide._ — P7, Sep 22 · [M7](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-07)
- Quotes withheld (no quote consent): P4; their evidence still counts. Sources: [M4](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-04)

#### F2. Admins skip the template gallery and build their first workflow from a blank canvas. — Medium confidence

Support: 3 of 7 participants (P2, P5, P7) · Contradict: 0 · Said: P7 · Did (observed): P2, P5

By segment (supporting/participants): Self-serve admins 2/4 · Sales-assisted teams 1/3

- Observed (notes, not verbatim speech): _He opened the template gallery, closed it after a few seconds, and built the workflow from a blank canvas._ — P2, Sep 15 · [M2](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-02)
- Observed (notes, not verbatim speech): _She skipped the template gallery and started from a blank canvas._ — P5, Sep 18 · [M5](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-05)
- Note excerpt (notes, not verbatim speech): _She did not open the template gallery and started from a blank canvas._ — P7, Sep 22 · [M7](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-07)

### RQ2 — What do admins expect from the first-week setup checklist?

#### F3. Admins want the setup checklist to show what their teammates still have to do. — Medium confidence

Support: 3 of 7 participants (P3, P5, P6) · Contradict: 0 · Said: P3, P5, P6 · Did (observed): none

By segment (supporting/participants): Self-serve admins 0/4 · Sales-assisted teams 3/3

_Note: stated only; no observed behaviour._

- “I need the checklist to show me what my team still has to do, not just my own steps.” — P3, Sep 16 · [M3](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-03)
- “I want the checklist to list single sign-on and who on my team hasn't logged in yet.” — P5, Sep 18 · [M5](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-05)
- Quotes withheld (no quote consent): P6; their evidence still counts. Sources: [M6](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-06)

#### F6. Admins expect single sign-on to be a step in the setup checklist. — Medium confidence

Support: 3 of 7 participants (P3, P5, P6) · Contradict: 1 (P2) · Said: P3, P5, P6 · Did (observed): none

By segment (supporting/participants): Self-serve admins 0/4 (1 contradict) · Sales-assisted teams 3/3

_Note: stated only; no observed behaviour._

- “And single sign-on has to be on it, our security review asks for it first.” — P3, Sep 16 · [M3](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-03)
- “I want the checklist to list single sign-on and who on my team hasn't logged in yet.” — P5, Sep 18 · [M5](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-05)
- Contradicting: “We don't need single sign-on. We're eleven people.” — P2, Sep 15 · [M2](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-02)
- Quotes withheld (no quote consent): P6; their evidence still counts. Sources: [M6](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-06)

#### F4. A progress bar would motivate admins to finish the setup checklist. — Low confidence

Support: 2 of 7 participants (P1, P4) · 2 only via leading questions (P1, P4) · Contradict: 1 (P5) · Said: P1, P4 · Did (observed): none

By segment (supporting/participants): Self-serve admins 2/4 · Sales-assisted teams 0/3 (1 contradict)

_Note: rests only on answers to leading questions; stated only; no observed behaviour._

- “Yeah, I guess a progress bar would be nice.” — P1, Sep 14 · [M1](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-01) — ⚠ answer to a leading question (“Wouldn't a progress bar make you more likely to finish the checklist?”), flagged by pattern; weaker evidence
- Contradicting: “I ignore progress bars, to be honest.” — P5, Sep 18 · [M5](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-05)
- Quotes withheld (no quote consent): P4; their evidence still counts. Sources: [M4](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-04)

### RQ3 — Who else gets involved before a workspace is rolled out to the team?

No finding for this question is supported by 2 or more participants.

## Jobs, pains, workarounds and desired outcomes

### Jobs to be done

- **F7** Get recurring team hand-offs running on their own, without chasing people or filing IT tickets. — High confidence · 4 participants (P1, P2, P4, P7)
  - “I want the Monday hand-offs between dispatch and billing to run without me chasing people, and without filing an IT ticket every time.” — P1, Sep 14 · [M1](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-01)

### Pains

- **F8** The credentials for the first data source are held by someone else in the company. — Medium confidence · 3 participants (P1, P3, P5)
  - “I asked \[name\] in finance for the login and waited two days.” — P1, Sep 14 · [M1](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-01)

### Current workarounds

- **F9** Keep the hand-off in a shared spreadsheet until the workflow has earned trust. — Medium confidence · 3 participants (P2, P4, P6)
  - “We still run the reminders from a shared spreadsheet. Until I trust the workflow, the spreadsheet stays.” — P2, Sep 15 · [M2](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-02)

### Desired outcomes

- **F10** See one workflow run end to end on day one. — Low confidence · 2 participants (P1, P5)
  - “Seeing one workflow run end to end on day one. Then I'd trust it.” — P1, Sep 14 · [M1](https://notes.granola.ai/d/sample-urs-01)

## Method and coverage

- **Interviews**: 7 included; 2 listed but excluded (all participants declined consent; internal debrief, not an interview).
- **Transcripts**: 6 read, 1 denied, 0 unavailable, 0 not requested.
- **Evidence**: 30 items (25 from transcripts, 5 from notes; 2 observed behaviour). 3 answers to leading questions (1 flagged by the question-wording pattern); 5 without the question's wording. 7 quotes withheld for consent; 0 not checked against source text because the text was not in the bundle.
- **Confidence** (distinct participants; answers to leading questions only count towards low): High = 4+ supporting and at least 50% of the 7 participants, with contradicting participants at most 25% of supporting; Medium = 3+; Low = 2+ in total; Contested = at least as many contradict as support; Single = fewer than 2, never shown as a pattern.
- **Anonymity**: participants appear as P1, P2… and sessions as M1, M2…; names, organisations and emails listed privately in the bundle (and the parts of listed full names) are replaced with [name] or [organisation], and email addresses, phone numbers and web addresses with a placeholder, in every displayed field. This is pattern-based and can miss unlisted names, nicknames, misspellings, look-alike letters and unusual formats; it can also blank ordinary words that match a listed name. Meeting links open the original notes, which are not anonymised.
- **Sources**: Meeting list: Granola, ok (folder: Onboarding interviews); Transcripts: Granola, partial.
- **Gaps**: transcripts partial (1 transcript denied by workspace policy).

_Internal research document. The agent extracted and coded the evidence; the script validated citations and quotes, counted participants, computed confidence and pseudonymised the text._
