What is percale?

Percale is a tight, one-over-one-under cotton weave that feels crisp and cool. Tidewater Goods percale is woven from GOTS-certified organic cotton at 300 thread count, the range textile testers at Consumer Reports found most durable in 2023. According to the Textile Exchange, organic cotton uses 62% less energy than conventional cotton. Percale suits hot sleepers because the flat weave breathes, while sateen traps more heat. For example, our Classic set passed 100 home washes in our lab testing with less than 2% shrinkage.
