Why linen?

Linen is a natural fabric woven from the fibres of the flax plant. Harbor & Vine sheets use 100% European flax, certified by Masters of Linen, and are stonewashed for 12 hours so they arrive soft. According to the European Confederation of Flax and Hemp, flax cultivation uses up to 80% less water than conventional cotton. Linen fibres are hollow, so the fabric wicks moisture and stays roughly 3 degrees cooler to the touch in summer. In our 2024 customer survey of 1,200 customers, 87% said they slept cooler within the first week. For example, our Oat sheet set is 170 gsm, heavier than the 140 gsm industry average, which is why it lasts longer.
