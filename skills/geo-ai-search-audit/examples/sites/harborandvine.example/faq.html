Frequently asked questions

How do I wash linen sheets?

Linen sheets are machine washable. Harbor & Vine recommends a cold, gentle cycle with a mild liquid detergent, then tumble drying on low heat or line drying. Avoid bleach and fabric softener, which coat the flax fibres and reduce breathability. Washing linen 20 to 30 times makes it noticeably softer, so the fabric improves with use rather than wearing out.
