## Method and limits

- Scores are heuristics from the page HTML, robots.txt and llms.txt. They are not rankings, citation counts or traffic from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude or Google AI features.
- Crawler access is inferred from robots.txt only. It shows what the site asks crawlers to do; firewalls, bot-management tools (e.g. CDN bot blocking) and crawler behaviour are not tested.
- Only the raw HTML is analysed. Content injected by JavaScript is not seen, which is also true for most AI crawlers.
- A sample of up to 5 page(s) per site was analysed, not the whole site.
- Brand authority, third-party mentions, reviews and E-E-A-T are not measured by this audit.
- llms.txt is a community proposal; whether a given AI engine reads it is not confirmed.
